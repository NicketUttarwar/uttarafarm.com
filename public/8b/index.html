A season with Uttara Farm

A sample path, not a rigid script. Timing flexes with monsoon, soil results, and what each village circle needs most.

  1. Step 1

    Meet

    Meet the circle, walk the plots, and agree which fields join turmeric or ginger for the coming season.

  2. Step 2

    Test

    Test kits and advisors read the land. Nutrition plans follow the soil, not a one-size packet.

  3. Step 3–4

    Grow

    Plant well, then mid-season irrigation, disease watch, and nutrition adjustments protect income.

  4. Step 5–6

    Sell

    Grade and bundle before the truck; produce moves toward bigger buyers so every farmer can keep improving.

FAQ

Still unsure? Reach Vijay and we will answer.

Who can join Uttara Farm?

Individual farmers, cooperative members, and contract growers across Vidarbha. Ralegaon is a special focus within the region.

Why start with turmeric and ginger?

Both can deliver strong returns and regular demand, but they need disciplined season-long care—so we wrap them in advice, soil insight, and market routes.

Do you only serve Ralegaon?

No. We serve all of Vidarbha. Ralegaon is a special focus, not our only geography.

Is this a commercial farm?

Uttara Farm is a social project. The aim is to raise yield and livelihood so every last rupee can reach the farmer.
